Run inventory work in Business Central from other systems and AI assistants.

Bifrost Inventory lets a warehouse system, an integration or an AI assistant carry out inventory tasks in Business Central: move stock between locations, build assembly items, track lots and serial numbers, reserve stock, count inventory and close the period. Every task runs through Business Central's own logic, exactly as if a user did it. It is an add-on to Bifrost Foundation.

Who it is for

Companies that run warehouse, e-commerce or production systems next to Business Central, and teams that want an assistant to help with day-to-day inventory work.

What it does

  • Transfer orders: create, release, reopen, preview and post transfers between locations, read their statistics, and undo a posted shipment or receipt.
  • Assembly orders: create, refresh lines, release, reopen, preview and post assembly orders, and undo a posted assembly.
  • Item tracking and reservations: assign or remove lot, serial and package numbers, see what is available per lot, and reserve or cancel stock for a demand.
  • Physical inventory: calculate expected quantities, record counts, preview and post the count journal.
  • Reclassification: check, preview and post reclassification journals.
  • Costing and periods: read item costs, run cost adjustment, post inventory cost to the general ledger, calculate revaluation, update unit prices, fix item applications, and close or reopen inventory periods.
  • Item attributes: read item attributes, give items attribute values and define new attributes with their options.

Control and safety

  • Each area can be turned on or off per company on one setup page.
  • Posting needs its own permission set, so a system can create and release documents without being able to post them.
  • Every request is checked before anything changes, and errors come back with a clear reason.

Bifrost Inventory does not replace Business Central or its extensions. It makes their data and business logic available to the people, routines and AI platforms your organisation already uses.
